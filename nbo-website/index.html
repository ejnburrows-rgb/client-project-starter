04 / OFFER
Automation with boundaries

Move the work. Keep judgment human.

Administrative movement can be automated. Legal decisions and consequential communications stay with the firm.

The system can handle

  • Acknowledgments and approved intake questions
  • Routing, scheduling and reminders
  • Document requests and status prompts
  • Pipeline visibility and routine handoffs

Your team keeps control of

  • Legal advice and eligibility decisions
  • Conflict review and engagement decisions
  • Fees, exceptions and sensitive judgment
  • Final approval of consequential communications

AI may support drafting, classification or summarization when appropriate and approved. It is infrastructure — not the promise.
